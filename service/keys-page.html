Keys for your TV

Movies, TV Shows, Search and prices on the TV come from three free services. Each needs a key that someone in this home makes, once. Paste each key here: the TV checks it with the service and keeps it. This page keeps nothing.

Making the keys is easier on a laptop. Open there, on the same Wi-Fi, and type the code the TV shows.

1. TMDB Needed for Movies, TV Shows and Search · free for personal use

How to get it (about 5 minutes)
  1. Make a free account at themoviedb.org/signup, then open the link in the email TMDB sends you.
  2. Open Settings › API. Choose to create a key, pick Developer, and accept the terms.
  3. Fill in the form. Type of use: Personal. For the three about the app, copy these:
    HomeTV Launcher
    https://github.com/rebeccamcfadden/hometv-launcher
    A home screen for our TV that shows where films and shows can be watched.
    The rest asks about you. Send it: the key is ready at once.
  4. Back on Settings › API, copy the API Read Access Token: the long one that starts with eyJ. Not the short “API Key”.
  5. Paste it below.

2. Watchmode Rent and buy prices · free: 2,500 credits a month

How to get it (about 2 minutes)
  1. Open api.watchmode.com/requestApiKey. No card is needed.
  2. Fill in your name and email; for what it’s for, “Prices on our home TV”. Send it.
  3. Copy your API key from the page it shows, or from your Watchmode account.
  4. Paste it below.

3. Movie of the Night More prices, when Watchmode has none · optional · free: 1,000 requests a month

How to get it (about 2 minutes)
  1. Sign up at developers.movieofthenight.com. No card is needed.
  2. On your dashboard, press Copy beside the API key. (A key from RapidAPI won’t work here.)
  3. Paste it below.

Keys go straight to the TV over your home network, and stay on it. The TV only ever shows their last four characters. The page works while the TV shows its API keys screen.
